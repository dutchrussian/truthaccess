After non-use, say or think "Signal" first before question - the first bit of movement is often just the channel waking up — a recognition of the on-switch, not an answer. Let the first one pass. Ask your question again if you didn't say signal and rushed in reading first movement as answer. The second response is the real one.

Best way to get a clear response is settle your breath, not in a rush or anxious, calm. Close your eyes to help settle and focus.
