If you understand that we are not our bodies and the brain is not the source of consciousness, you can understand the signal.

Your body is operated by your higher self, for easiness sake you can refer to it to anything that resonates for you now - Oversoul, Soul, Awareness, All that it is, God, Source, Consciousness, the greater reality. Whatever floats your boat – for practical purposes of extracting Truth it doesn't matter. What matter is that you go beyond the ego self. In our current stage of evolution, tools and possibilities are opening up to be more connected to levels of reality beyond your temporary idea of a limited body bound self.

Best way to get a clear response is to briefly close your eyes, settle your breath, not be intoxicated, rushed, or anxious state.

The signal is a way to extract truth from other levels, without meditation, without intermediaries, directly via your body.

It is a non-intrusive bodily response to a statement or questions.

It returns one of three answers:

- **TRUE**
- **FALSE**
- **NO ANSWER (BLANK)**

If used correctly, it bypasses belief, logic, and preference. It is not intuition and it is not wishful thinking. With practice it becomes quieter and clearer.

The Signal was installed in bodies from 1975 onwards by the entities helping us in our incarnation. About 75% of people born after 1974 can get it to work. For those who can't – either they are still working out some things that are preventing the functioning for now (we will release a meditation that fixes this in a month) or their incarnation has a specific task that excludes any metaphysical interests. For some people it also activates only 1 week after saying the activation phrase.
