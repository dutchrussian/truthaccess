**Have a clear goal instead of open-ended requests:** "Is this car in good order or Is this a lemon and a poor purchase?" is better than "Should I buy this car?"

If you want to ask "Should I take this job?" -> get clear on where do you want to arrive first, what do you want? Answer that first and then you might get better questions, depending on your priorities. Is it 1) to provide for your family as soon as possible or 2) a job that is aligned with your joy and / or optimal path crafted by your higher self. We will let it up to you to ask the realistic possibility of combining several asks from the activity at a given time in your life.

You can run several options available to you through true/ false if you are faced with a choice.

**Ask about next steps, not outcomes.** "Is the most truth-aligned next action to call her today" will answer. "Will we end up together" will likely bring in your ego.

**Address it.** Directing the question to your true self, universe or guide rather than firing it into the air, helps bypass the ego.

**Ask for truth. Use the actual word truth.** Said with sincerity it goes past your mind-ego complex defences towards truth.

**One thing at a time.** Split "should I leave and move to Bristol" into two questions.

**Close your eyes**. A way to focus inward and bypass the ego
