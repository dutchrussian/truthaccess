Calibrate one more time, eyes closed, mind quiet.

- **For TRUE.** Ask something you know is so — *"Is my name my name?"* Wait for the response. That's your yes.
- **For FALSE.** Ask something you know is not so. Wait. That's your no.

No answer / Blank is information. For some it has its own movement/ signal, for some it is just silence and no movement. It usually means one of the following:

- the question is not yours to have;
- it is beyond what you can hold right now;
- it would harm you or someone else to know;
- it would violate another person's privacy;
- or it is simply yours to decide — the Signal handing the choice back to you as that is a part of your journey now.
- It doesn't matter;
- The question needs rephrasing.
