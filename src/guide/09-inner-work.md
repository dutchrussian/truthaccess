If you want to live closer to what's true, the Signal becomes more than a convenience. It becomes a way to evolve yourself towards realising your true nature and undoing generations of beliefs that are untrue and hamper your flourishing.

**The thing you're up against.** The moment you point this at your own deepest beliefs, the temporary part of you that runs on fear and control has something to lose — and it fights. So as you go deeper you may get *more* false answers, not fewer. This is not the Signal failing. It is the Signal showing you where you are still holding on and need cleaning up of deep-seated, false, beliefs. This work has many layers and takes time and dedication. Be kind to yourself.

**Your tools.** Sincerity — wanting the truth more than wanting to be right. And the clean second question — *"Did my ego just lie to me?"* — asked because you mean it.

The Signal can surface a fear on the move; it can rarely dissolve one on its own. The deep, old ones need another modality for most people — a steady meditation practice, or direct writing.
