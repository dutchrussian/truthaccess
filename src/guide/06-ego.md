This channel allows you to speak to higher level of intelligence. The ego can interfere and use the same channel to assert its power over you, but there are ways to get past it. If you are going to start scanning bible verses for truth after having spent your life as a devout Christian, you can bet your ego is going to be up in arms defending your life long nurtured persona.

Remember how we said your higher self runs the body. It does, but it usually defers to the temporary, unreliable structure that is called the ego or mind. You can read more about what ego is [here](/faq/#what-is-ego). If the interests of ego and higher self align – the signal returns truth without extra measures. Where there is any sign of attachment to a preferred outcome to defend one's personality, even subconscious, the ego can change the response last minute and you might get a false positive if you are not 100% committed to and sincere about getting the truth about the issue.

Some ego desires: do as little as possible / laziness, quick rewards without effort, wants to predict future far out, wants others to think of it in a certain way, asks from fear-based beliefs or anxiety, engages in gossip, conspiracy theories for the purpose of perpetuating excitement / fear and not for the purpose of finding out what is true and relevant for the self

All practical spheres of life can be interacted with from the positioning of truth or from the position of ego/ conditioning. This can be very subtle: at your core you might generally be indifferent about a subject, but if you just spent an evening defending your view to your friends, not all parts of you might be ready to let truth pass.

A subtle example: Your old cat is sick; you love it, but an unacknowledged part of you doesn't want the hassle anymore. You ask if the cat is dying or needs the vet and your ego hijacks the signal and tells you he is dying, let him die peacefully alone. In truth, the vet could have fixed a simple issue, and the cat could have lived another 3 years. Depending on your desire for truth over convenience, it is best to double check with this question "Did a part of me just lie to me?"

We are not advocating "killing the ego" or having a negative attitude towards it. An ego-based belief or fear should be seen as a tool that might have been useful in the past to protect you, but you have matured past it and want to explore a more potent, adult tool - truth. Remember – you are the boss, you decide when you are ready to stretch your boundaries. It all depends on how much you want the truth vs protecting an established idea of the truth.

Below we assume you want the truth on a given subject. To get it, you can use the following tools to bypass the ego defences that want to keep you its slave.

### Truth is a key word.

If used sincerely and not mechanically, it gives ammunition to your higher self to bypass the ego defences. Say the word – specifically ask for Truth on the matter.

### Ask past your own self

Direct the question outward of what you currently know yourself to be (mostly it's the body, ego and self). This means ask question to your higher self, oversoul, your guide, or Source — rather than to the everyday self that wants a particular outcome. Your base experience identifies with the ego; truth comes from beyond it. As your practice matures and you are living less from ego, the need for this diminishes for most questions.

### The clean second question

A great 2nd question is: **"Did my ego just lie to me?"** Asked sincerely, it shows your desire for truth — and that desire tends to open the real answer by answering Yes to "Did my ego just lie to me" or "Did my ego just interfere?"

### State it in the positive

Ask the positive version first, not the fearful negative. *"This is safe for me"* before *"This is going to ruin me."* Fear pulls the strongest distortion, and a negative framing invites your ego mind complex to distort the response. The Signal is biased away from reinforcing fear and negative belief unless it is genuinely necessary — your ego, on the other hand, loves a negative sentence, because it feeds the worry.

See it as a tool to learn to ask better, more sincere questions. If after an answer you get an inkling to double check the answer – double check. It's that unintrusive ping from your higher self to catch your ego out. The bonus is that the more you do it, the more advanced topics you get access to and the more use from the signal you get. They don't give fire to children for a reason, but if you prove you became an adult, then why not?

If you get tricked by your ego – don't get offended! It is only an ego that sees this as a problem and not a learning opportunity.

Bonus tip: Ask "Would xyz thought or action be aligned with my soul or ego?" This shows you are serious to learn the distinction and align yourself properly.

> All doors are opened with the key being sincere desire for truth and alignment with intelligence of the higher self.
