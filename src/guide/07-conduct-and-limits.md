The Signal works on sincerity. Use it for mockery, showing off, sarcasm, or in anger, and it will not work.

- It does not return clean answers when the body is under the influence of alcohol, recreational drugs, or strong painkillers and opioids — roughly twelve hours to clear.
- It will not perform. It will not find hidden objects or guess numbers to impress a room. Asked to show off, it goes blank or will give false positive, so you learn not to do it again.
- It does not open other people's private thoughts, past, or future to you.
- It works for the past, the present, and sometimes the short-term, personally relevant future — where it doesn't harm you to know.
- It returns blank on anything that would harm you or someone else.
