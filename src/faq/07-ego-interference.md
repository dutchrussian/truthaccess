If you are going to start scanning bible verses for truth after having spent your life as a devout Christian, you can bet your ego is going to be up in arms defending your life long nurtured persona. If you have spent a significant amount of money and time on a project, a part you feel is you is going to want to hold on to hope, when you ask if moving on is the more aligned action for your overall wellbeing. When you have a fight with your partner on a subject, you really want to want truth over preserving the idea that you were right / smarter, to get the truth past the ego barrier. These are just some examples.

Mostly the ego becomes really active when one is working on spiritual evolution and living more out of soul than out of ego. When a person is fully self-realized, working in the physical whilst not being of the physical, there is no room for the ego and it dissolves. The ego knows this and is patient, sly and resourceful and can use convoluted methods to trick and fool us into thinking we have mastered it. It infuses us with feelings of self-consciousness, failure, depression, success, materialism, status and pride. Success and pride are two emotions the ego uses with skill because we can feel these emotions as a result of being pleased with our spiritual progress. And, when it uses these to the best of its ability we can "think" we are making spiritual progress when in effect we are not. Notice how some of the most spiritual people also have the biggest egos! The very moment we think that we have mastered the ego we have lost the battle and the ego is back in control of us and our thoughts, and, we lose some of our spiritual progression.

We are not advocating "killing the ego" or having a negative attitude towards it. An ego-based belief or fear should be seen as a tool that might have been useful in the past to protect you, but you have matured past it and want to explore a tool that is much more potent - truth. Remember – you are the boss, you decide when you are ready to stretch your boundaries. It all depends on how much you want the truth vs protecting an established idea of the truth. Here we assume you want the truth on a given subject. To get it, you can use [the following tools](/wiki/#ego) to get truth on a given subject despite still living mostly out of ego. If you are not sincere enough on a certain topic – you are just not going to use them.
