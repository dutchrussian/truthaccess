This can have 3 reasons. One – your higher self and/ or guide is very enthusiastic to finally be in touch with you and wants to encourage you, they don't do half baked responses when it is something that would make you shine. Two – your ego has indeed overtaken the channel and is skewing it. For this we recommend you read [the part on ego in our wiki.](/wiki/#ego) Three - the answer is a strong yes to a properly framed, sincere and relevant question.
