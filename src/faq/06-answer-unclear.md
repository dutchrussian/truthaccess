First the basics – are your eyes closed when you ask? Are you calm/ breathing normally? Have you stopped / running / walking etc? Have you not drunk alcohol or used intoxicants in the past 12 hours? If that is covered then check your phrasing:

Are you asking open ended or unclear questions? For example, Should I continue working with this colleague or not is better framed with your goal like – is it more aligned with my highest creative expression to leave the partnership? Or to work out our issues? If you want to know the next step, you must specify where you want to go directionally.

Are you trying to belittle yourself in any way? The signal will not indulge any negative self talk, it is a practical tool aimed at revealing to you your own power and divinity, not entertaining any parts that are interested in putting you down.

Lastly, your ego might be messing things up. Ask: did my ego just lie to me in that last answer? Ask for truth and direct the question to your guide, higher self or Source - a "higher" aspect, that you don't identify with day to day as a lived experience.
