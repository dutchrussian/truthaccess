The ego is a creation, a temporary creation. It is created as a result of an aspect (soul) of our Over Soul (or Higher Self) being separated out from the Over Soul and projected into the lowest frequencies associated with our multiversal environment, to experience learn and evolve in an accelerated way. To experience this evolutionary acceleration the aspect (soul) must enter into the frequencies in the way they are best experienced, as if it were part of them. To do so it needs to associate itself with a vehicle which it can animate energetically. This vehicle is, in our instance, the human form. The condition of being in "partial communication", or even almost "zero communication" is what creates the ego. The ego is therefore a condition where the sentience associated with the energies that are the aspect (soul) associate themselves in an isolated or individualised way with the gross physical aspect of the human form, the "body". The ego is a temporary or transient condition because it can only exist whilst the human form is operational (alive), and, when we are not self-realized. When the human form demises (dies) it dissolves, although the experiential memories of what we are whilst incarnate are not lost because they are always transmitted to the Oversoul in parallel with the experience itself. The ego can also be dissolved if we work on our spiritual development and regain contact with our higher self. The ego knows this and does everything in its power to stay in control, in power, as long as it can, and so will work hard on arresting our spiritual progression.
